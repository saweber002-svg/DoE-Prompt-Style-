# 07 — Unknowns & Lab Notes (the measurement roadmap)

Everything here is **unknown in every public source** (see research report §12–13). Each item is a Lab Notes task in the app: measure it in-game (damage numbers display exists since Sept 2026), then land the value in `data/` with provenance. Until then it renders as `?` — never estimated.

| # | Unknown | Why it matters | How to resolve |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Every ring's numeric value** | Rings are core calculator inputs | Read values off physical rings in-game; user-entered fields until a captured table exists |
| 2 | Poison DoT (tick damage / rate / duration) | Poison is a real damage contributor | Timed in-game tests on a flat target (Wasp) |
| 3 | EXO per-level values (levels 1–5) | Only maxed-build values are known | Screenshot the EXO Point Board at each level |
| 4 | Mimic zones (and any image of its monster form) | Roster member with zero data | In-game encounter capture |
| 5 | Swing/throw strength curve | Documented to exist, zero quantification | Controlled throws at a flat target with damage numbers on |
| 6 | Spectral Echo delay + proc chance (+ undead-only?) | A whole damage event outside the chain | Frame-counted in-game capture |
| 7 | Difficulty & co-op scaling numbers | Affects real-world hit values | Tier-by-tier / player-count tests |
| 8 | Axe handling contradiction (slide ×2 vs narration ×3/×6) | One factor, two readings | Direct in-game grip test |
| 9 | First-strike placement in the formula | Official ×3, unplaced by testers | Unaware-target test inside a known chain |
| 10 | Backstab vs Scout's: which wins the non-stack | Changes optimal ring pairing | Paired-ring test from behind on a head-zone target |
| 11 | Proc-rate ring amounts (Hexweaver / Blacksmith / Micro Amplifier) | All frequency tables are baselines until known | Large-sample proc counting with/without each ring |
| 12 | Base crit chance with no perks (if any) | Affects every crit calculation | Long no-perk hit sample |
