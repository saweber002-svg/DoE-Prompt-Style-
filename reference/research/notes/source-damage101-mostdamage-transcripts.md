## Damage 101 facts
- Enemy zones: every enemy has 2-3 damage zones: standard zone (no extra damage "outside of weapon perks or exo suit"), weak point (x2 or x3), targeted zone (where rings apply damage).
- ROUNDING (guest tester Pimp Nokio): displayed damage numbers are rounded — "when it says four times damage, it's not four times damage. It's 4.05. The multipliers are rounded up to the nearest tenth. The damage itself is rounded up to the nearest whole."
- Skeletal Warrior: chest = standard (no bonus), head = NO bonus, waistband = x3 weak point (wraps entire waist).
- Test axe described as "fire, criticals, explosions axe": Fire, Explosions/Area Damage each x2 modifier; yellow damage number = critical hit x3. "Weapon perks roll simultaneously and then incrementally add a multiplier."
- "Arrows can typically only have one or two damage modifiers, where melee weapons can roll all their perks simultaneously, where there we had a times nine."
- All enemies have an armor value. Unarmored-type enemy (archer/sorcerer class): head = x3 weak point.
- Two Scout's Signets (headshot rings) vs two Sundering Rings (armor-piercing) tested: two of the same ring CAN be worn. Sundering applies its multiplier on any body hit vs armored; "damage dealt to weak points will be dealt double when dealing with the sundering ring"; Scout+Sundering stack on headshots. Worked modifiers: x6 (Sundering vs skeletal warrior waist), x9 (sorcerer head with both ring types), x9.5 displayed (zombie headshot, both rings), x6 / 6.3 (imp head), x10 (Uber Mage head). Armor value differs per enemy and changes Sundering results.
- Zombie: damage NULLIFICATION on torso and limbs; head weak point x3.
- Imp: horns nullify damage (headshot still possible); weak point = neck/chin, only x2.
- Skeletal Boss: damage nullification everywhere except face; face is vulnerable but is NOT a weak point.
- Uber Mage: head x3, chest x2, limbs x1 standard, backpack = nullifies all damage; it teleports/disappears when hit even in a null zone.
- Greatsword one hand vs two hands: "using two hands will double the damage, but that's actually sometimes less damage than you would otherwise be doing in one hand more consistently" (variability).
- A test used a 100 base damage bow and an "undead ring" (enemy-specific ring — excluded by scope; existence noted). Legendary versions of Scout/Sundering rings give bigger numbers than non-legendary.

## Most Damage facts
- EXO PERKS NAMED: "the exo perks pull back and power" — increase damage "by 1.3 and 1.5, respectively" (narration ambiguous vs slide: slide lists [POWER/PULLBACK] = 0.5 and [ATTACK POWER] = 0.35; likely Attack Power x1.35, Power/Pullback x1.5 — FLAGGED ambiguity).
- "situational exo perks such as the might perk, which increases throwing damage, and the pierce perk triggered by thrusting your weapon. And both of these will give you that times two modifier." → EXO Might = x2 throwing damage; EXO Pierce = x2 on thrusts.
- "Both your exo criticals and your weapon perk criticals can trigger at the same time and increase the chance, making that your best opportunity to get the highest modifier at times 9." → EXO Criticals exists (slide: [CRITICAL/IMPALE] 3 at 40% chance); double crit = x3 x3 = x9.
- Radius effects: Area Damage and Explosions x2, stack with Fire element x2.
- Fire display quirk: "Whenever fire triggers, it will appear red even if you hit a critical hit" — yellow crit numbers understate max.
- Worked build: "wielding a 130 fire, explosions, criticals, and exo crit" weapon maxes at "nearly 95,000 damage" (matches calculator table: 130 x 730.1 = 94,910 head FCCE).
- PERK COUNT BY BASE DAMAGE: "max damage will erase a perk. At 150, you only get one perk and an element. Where at 130, that is the maximum number you can get two perks and an element on your spear and swords. We'll get into the axe later because that's weird."
- Highest base damage weapons: spear, side sword, long sword.
- Stab perk: both swords; +50% damage "regardless of your level's loot tier... even at level one"; side sword stab always active; longsword must be used two-handed for full benefit (narration ties the x2 to two hands = handling bonus).
- Handling bonuses: spear two hands = flat x2; hammer x2; greatsword x2; AXE = x3 in one hand, x6 in two hands (CONTRADICTS the same video's slide which lists axe handling = 2 — internal inconsistency flagged); dagger cannot be two-handed, stabbing does x1.5. Handling bonus also applies to enemy weak zones (multiplies).
- Throwing: "throwing your weapons will offer you no additional damage bonus. But the attack power perk and the critical perk will both trigger whether you are in close range or throwing your weapon." Attack Power preferred over Throw Damage because it works at both ranges.
- Perk activation: melee — all perks roll per hit; double-crit + fire + explosions together = 1% of the time. Ranged — "only two perks can be active at any given time, not including always on perks like attack power or pullback." Bow: fire + criticals can trigger simultaneously; fire overrides any other element including the Slowing perk. Crossbow: a lit critical bolt is overridden to fire damage only (crit lost); fire continues to override elements/slowing.
- Arrows/bolts can be lit from wall torches (sconces; not candles/pitfires) and even by a Mythic Hammer; lit arrow deals radius x2 damage; you cannot see the fire perk on another player's arrow. Mythic Bow therefore has an extra damage modifier other mythics lack.
