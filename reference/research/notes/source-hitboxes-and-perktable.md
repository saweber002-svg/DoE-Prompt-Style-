# Hit boxes video + wiki perk table completion (verified 2026-10-21)

## All hit boxes in dungeons of eternity — Dungeons Demystified
URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VpAyfSrzPp4 — published Oct 9, 2026, 644 views, 8:11. Video / community-tested. Chapters: Introduction to mechanics 0:00; Standard enemy weak points 1:07; Boss enemy breakdown 5:07; Conclusion 7:34.
- Enemies have 1-3 damage zones; some zones nullify damage; some enemies have unique modifiers.
- In-game setting: pause menu toggle "show damage dealt" enables damage numbers display.
- Small spider: x3 everywhere. Large spider: legs x1, thorax x2, face x3.
- Skeletal warrior & skeletal minion: only waistline bonus (x3 per Damage 101); head and torso standard.
- Archer & mage: head x3, body standard.
- Large scorpion: limbs standard, head or tail x2, claws nullify.
- Zombie: body/limbs nullify (dismemberment possible at joints), head x3; fire/area/explosions radius can envelop the head.
- Imp: horns nullify (horns removable), chin/neck x2 only (no x3).
- Blind Octoculus: x6 modifier wherever hit; no headshot bonus.
- Wasps & bats: no extra modifiers. Bats usually show only 2 damage even on crit; area damage (fire/explosions/area) overrides this.
- Lost Souls (flying skulls): x2 overall; headshot ring adds nothing.
- Slimes: x3 all around.
- Uber Mage (boss): upper chest x2, head x3, backpack nullifies; disappears on any hit. (Damage 101 said stomach = standard; this video's wording could be read as stomach x2 — minor wording discrepancy, flagged.)
- Doomer ("Chungus"): body x2, limbs x1, head x3, zones wrap front/back. When it roars it releases a "quad potion" (Quad Damage Potion); shooting its mouth triggers the roar — farmable.
- Octoculus (boss): eyes give no extra damage (reduce its bombardment at HP thresholds); body x2 all around; headshot rings do not proc on it.
- Queen Wasp: no modifiers anywhere.
- Skeletal Boss: face is only vulnerable point, body nullifies; face takes NO extra multiplier, but DOES take headshot-ring damage.
