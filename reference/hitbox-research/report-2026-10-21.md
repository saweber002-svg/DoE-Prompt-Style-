## Summary

- **No per-zone damage multiplier table (head ×N / chest ×N / legs ×N) is published anywhere public** — not in Othergate's patch notes, not on the Forsaken Planet community wiki, not on the Fandom wiki, and not in any Steam guide, review, Reddit thread or press article found. Zone damage in this game is documented only qualitatively (weak spots, invulnerable bodies, armor), with exactly **three hard numbers** in the whole corpus, none of them a body-zone multiplier:
  1. **First strike: an unaware enemy takes 3× damage on the first hit** [OFFICIAL, Tomb Extraction, 21 Sep 2026].
  2. **Criticals weapon perk: 20–32% chance to critically strike for 300% damage (×3)** [COMMUNITY wiki, Perks page] — a weapon perk, not a body zone.
  3. **Spectral Echo perk: damage echoes doing double damage (×2)** [OFFICIAL, Sep 2026].
- **The owner-supplied "Megamage Head ×3 / Chest ×2 / Legs ×1" is UNVERIFIED.** The name "Megamage" appears **nowhere** in the indexed web. The game's boss mage is the **Uber Mage**; the regular mage is the **Skeleton Mage**. No source gives any mage (or any enemy) a head/chest/legs multiplier split. Plausible conflation sources for a "×3 head" memory: the Criticals perk (300%), the first-strike 3× bonus, and the Sep 2026 damage-numbers UI, which shows multipliers in-game.
- **Zone structure is real but qualitative.** Documented zone behaviour: zombies take **no body damage and die instantly to head hits** [COMMUNITY/Fandom]; the Skeleton Boss can (per wiki + a Steam review) **only be damaged via its face opening** (crossbow bolts pierce its armor); the Octoculus Boss's **eight floating eyes are strikable weak points that weaken its defences**; arms are a distinct damage zone (a hit on a frozen enemy's weapon "applies damage like hitting its arm" [OFFICIAL]); skeletons **cannot block attacks to their feet/legs** [COMMUNITY/Fandom]; official patch notes confirm headshots exist as a concept ("Fixed Doomer headshots" [OFFICIAL]) and that hit **colliders** are tuned per enemy ("Skeleton Boss hit colliders adjusted", "Adjusted Scorpion hit boxes") — but no values are ever published.
- **Since 21 Sep 2026 the game itself displays the data**: Tomb Extraction added toggleable floating **damage numbers with hit multipliers shown, gold critical hits and blue multiplier tints** [OFFICIAL]. That in-game UI — not any document — is now the only authoritative way to read zone multipliers, and no player has yet published measurements extracted from it that search can find.

## General Hitbox System

### What is officially documented [OFFICIAL — othergate.com/updates, fetched in full]
- **Damage numbers & multipliers display (Tomb Extraction, 21 Sep 2026):** "Damage Numbers rising off enemies (toggleable), with hit multipliers shown, gold critical hits and blue multiplier tints." Steam version: "Damage points and multipliers rising off enemies (toggleable)." UploadVR (26 Sep 2026): "Damage numbers and multipliers now pop up for enemies. This can be toggled off."
- **First-strike multiplier (21 Sep 2026):** "First strike bonus: an unaware enemy takes 3x damage on the first hit."
- **Headshots exist (21 Sep 2026 fix list):** "Fixed Doomer headshots." — proves a distinct head hitbox with special handling on at least the Doomer; no multiplier given.
- **Collider tuning is per-enemy (Blizzards and Blades, 12 Dec 2025):** "Skeleton Boss hit colliders have been adjusted." (Steam's wording for the boss elsewhere: "Skeleton Knight boss".)
- **Scorpion hit boxes (Difficulty Bonus, 31 Jan 2025):** "Adjusted Scorpion hit boxes and made it turn a little faster."
- **Arms are a zone (Longswords & Traps, 2 May 2024):** "Hitting a frozen enemy's weapon applies damage like hitting its arm." — arm hits are processed as their own zone, and a held weapon counts as the arm.
- **Frozen-enemy shattering (Oct 2023–Oct 2024):** frozen enemies are "smashable"; "Fixed hits against frozen enemy weapons not shattering the enemies."
- **Soft vs hard bodies (17 Oct 2024):** "Improved the impact particles against soft bodied enemies (Zombies, Imps, etc)" — internal body-type distinction, no numbers.
- **Boss freeze rule (Jan 2024):** "Boss enemies can no longer be frozen… hitting a boss with an ice staff or ice weapon will still do damage."
- **Armored enemies (Sep 2026):** Tomb Breaker Hammer "works well against armored enemies"; Crypt Hammer "is a one-hit kill on ordinary skeletons (not guards)" and "bashes skeletons into walls where they shatter"; Skull Dagger "breaks when used on a guard."
- **Explosions vs bosses (Sep 2026 fix):** "Fixed explosions sometimes doing no damage to bosses."
- **Parry coverage (Sep 2026 fix):** "Fixed parrying not working on Mages, Doomers and Zombies."

### Community damage model [COMMUNITY — Forsaken Planet wiki]
- **Player damage formula (Combat System / New Players Guide pages, index):** damage dealt = the weapon's damage number, scaled by swing/throw strength; the scale factor depends on weapon type — crossbows ≈1×, Long Swords ≈2×. These are **weapon** scales, not body-zone multipliers. The in-game tutorial does not quantify swing strength.
- **Criticals perk (Perks page, fetched in full):** "Gives 20-32% chance to critically strike for 300% damage" across weapon tiers — the game's crit system is perk-based, not zone-based, as far as any document states.
- **Class-damage perks (Perks page, fetched):** Undead / Monster / Critter / Sorcerer / Elemental / Elite Damage perks add +5–35% damage against that enemy class across 7 weapon tiers. Explosions/Area Damage perks: 200%. Stab Damage: +50%. These are the only systematic damage modifiers the wiki documents — all attacker-side, none zone-side.
- **Toughness tiers (Enemy Toughness page, fetched):** five tiers — Light (yellowish), Medium (light green), Heavy (deep red), Elite (bright red), Legendary (bright purple). Toughness scales health, attack power and **damage resistance** — i.e., a per-enemy damage-reduction stat exists, but no values are published. (An older wiki revision describes the colours slightly differently: duller = weaker, red = Elite, bright purple = Legendary; bright red/blue/green can instead signal Fire/Ice/Poison elemental enemies.)
- **Enemy classes (Forsaken homepage, index):** Critters (biome creeps), Monsters (large), Undead, Sorcerers (mages). Perk-rebalance note [OFFICIAL, Sep 2026]: "Loot Goblin and Imps are monsters, scorpions are critters."

### Enemy damage TO the player (context only) [COMMUNITY]
Forsaken's Combat System page charts Light-tier enemy damage as % of player health (humanoid melee 8–16%, arrow 8%, scorpion pinch 25%, stinger 8%, bee explode 50%). This is incoming damage, not hitbox data.

## Per-Enemy Hitbox Findings

### Skeletons (Minion / Warrior / Archer)
- **Zones:** head / body / arms / legs-feet are implied by generic systems (headshot fixes are Doomer-specific; arm-zone rule is generic). **Legs/feet: skeletons cannot block attacks to their feet or legs** [COMMUNITY — Fandom main page, fetched]. Higher-difficulty skeletons wear armor "but this does not stop you from attacking them"; they block weapon attacks otherwise.
- **Multipliers:** NOT DOCUMENTED anywhere.
- **Mechanics:** shatter when frozen and smashed, and when bashed into walls by the Crypt Hammer (one-hit kill on ordinary skeletons) [OFFICIAL Sep 2026]; skeleton "gibs" stick to axes briefly [OFFICIAL Oct 2023]; fully armored Minions can parry [COMMUNITY].
- **Confidence:** zones — medium (Fandom is stale but specific); multipliers — none exist publicly.

### Skeleton Boss / "Skeleton Knight Boss"
- **Zones:** **Face opening is the weak spot — per the wiki, "the only real weak spot for melee"**; its plate armor "shrug[s] off sword strikes and arrow fire with ease," but **crossbow bolts can pierce its armor** [COMMUNITY — Forsaken Skeleton Boss page, rev 26 May 2025, fetched in full]. A top Steam review independently says Skeleton Knight bosses take damage **only** "by hitting them in the face" [COMMUNITY — steamcommunity.com/app/3189340/reviews, index].
- **Colliders:** "Skeleton Boss hit colliders have been adjusted" [OFFICIAL, 12 Dec 2025] — no values.
- **Multipliers:** NOT DOCUMENTED (face = effectively the only damaging zone per community sources; no ×N published).
- **Other:** Immunity: Icing (can be poisoned/slowed); Weakness: Undead Damage perk; attacks Swing Weapon / Stomp / Throw Fireball; drops Coins 65% / Health Potion 20% / Key 15% [COMMUNITY].
- **Confidence:** face weak point — **high** (two independent community sources + official collider tuning).

### Zombies
- **Zones:** **Body = no damage; head = instant kill.** Fandom (fetched): zombies "take no damage when attacked on the body and die instantly when you attack their head." Dismemberment is systemic: arms/legs can be chopped off; legless zombies crawl; armless zombies attack with the shoulder; a two-handed spear can pin them to wall/ground [COMMUNITY — Fandom main page].
- **Multipliers:** effectively head = lethal / body = 0 in Fandom's account, but **no numeric multipliers published**, and the newer Forsaken wiki has no equivalent Zombie page text retrievable — treat the Fandom claim as community-reported, unverified against current builds.
- **Other:** "soft bodied" [OFFICIAL]; parry fix mentions Zombies [OFFICIAL Sep 2026]; Doomer is a zombie mini-boss variant (below).
- **Confidence:** medium-low (single, stale source; Fandom itself defers to Forsaken Planet as the current wiki).

### The Doomer
- **Zones:** **Head zone confirmed officially**: "Fixed Doomer headshots" [OFFICIAL, 21 Sep 2026]. No other zones described.
- **Multipliers:** NOT DOCUMENTED.
- **Other:** "Doomer Zombie mini-boss (big guy) health has been nerfed. His vomit attack now has a special surprise" [OFFICIAL, 31 Jan 2025]; "He's a tank," added Dec 2024 with Death! and Tentacles; may detonate on death; rarely spits out a Quad Damage Potion [COMMUNITY — Forsaken The Doomer page via index snippets; page itself not re-fetched].
- **Confidence:** head hitbox exists — high (official). Everything else — low/none.

### Mages — Skeleton Mage and Uber Mage ("Megamage")
- **Skeleton Mage** [COMMUNITY — Forsaken page, fetched in full]: Sorcerer class; all realms; Weakness: Sorcerer Damage perk; Immunity: none; fights with a fireball staff; summons skeleton warriors and critters. **The page contains no hitbox zones and no multipliers.**
- **Uber Mage** [COMMUNITY — Forsaken via index snippets]: boss-level sorcerer in heavier armor than the Skeleton Mage; **teleports after being attacked, often behind players**; summons waves of skeletons and critters; high-tier runs. Officially: respawn time and melee damage reduced Oct 2023; a bug let Uber Mages "become invincible" (fixed May 2024); parry fix names "Mages" (Sep 2026).
- **Zones / multipliers for either mage: NOT DOCUMENTED.** No head/chest/legs split appears in any source.
- **"Megamage" verdict:** the word returns **zero** relevant results web-wide. The companion owner's Head ×3 / Chest ×2 / Legs ×1 split **cannot be corroborated** and should ship in the app labelled *unverified / owner-observed*, if at all. See Summary for likely conflations (Crit perk 300%, first-strike 3×, in-game multiplier UI).
- **Confidence:** high that no public documentation exists (exhaustive negative search); the claim itself remains possible as undocumented game behaviour — since Sep 2026 it is directly testable in-game via the damage-numbers UI.

### Spiders (Small Spider / Spider; incl. Kamikaze and Frost/Ice variants)
- **Zones/multipliers:** NOT DOCUMENTED. Officially only: "Adjusted Scorpion hit boxes" refers to scorpions, not spiders; for spiders: "Reduced Elite Bat and Spider health" (Jan 2024); "3s cooldown to getting webbed by a large spider" and "Ice Enemies moved to Tier 3" (Jan 2026); spiders spawn from breakables; Arachnophobia Mode swaps spider models (Jul 2024); Frost/Ice spiders run away after freezing a player (May 2024).
- **Confidence:** n/a — nothing public.

### Scorpions (Small / Scorpion / Scorpion King)
- **Zones:** hit boxes exist and were tuned — "Adjusted Scorpion hit boxes" [OFFICIAL, 31 Jan 2025] — but no zone breakdown or values.
- **Multipliers:** NOT DOCUMENTED. Classified as **critters** [OFFICIAL Sep 2026]. Incoming damage (community chart): pinch 25%, stinger 8% of player HP at Light tier.
- **Confidence:** hitbox existence — high (official); structure — undocumented.

### Wasps / Bees (Tiny–Large; Wasp Boss)
- **Zones/multipliers:** NOT DOCUMENTED. Officially: "Wasp Boss speed (-) and attack damage (-)" (Oct 2023). Community chart: bee explode = 50% of player HP (incoming).
- **Confidence:** n/a.

### Bats
- **Zones/multipliers:** NOT DOCUMENTED. Fandom (fetched): bats "die from even the weakest of attacks" — effectively 1 HP behaviour. Officially: Elite Bat health reduced Jan 2024 ("the bats were OP").
- **Confidence:** low-medium (stale Fandom + official balance note agree they're fragile).

### Imps (Minion / Warrior / Ranged / Kamikaze-Bomber)
- **Zones/multipliers:** NOT DOCUMENTED. "Soft bodied" [OFFICIAL]. Imp Ranged fights with dual hatchets (swing/lunge/throw) [COMMUNITY — Forsaken Imp Ranged page, index]. Bomber Imp damage reduced Jan 2024 [OFFICIAL]. Removed from Difficulty Tiers 1–2 (May 2024) [OFFICIAL]. Classified as **monsters** [OFFICIAL Sep 2026].
- **Confidence:** n/a.

### Golem (incl. Ice Golem, Magma Golem)
- **Zones/multipliers:** NOT DOCUMENTED. Boss-event enemy (Golem or Octoculus Boss chamber, Tier 3 chest reward) [COMMUNITY — Forsaken Dungeon Generation page, index]. Throws its hovering stones [OFFICIAL Dec 2023]. Ice Golem added Dec 2023 [OFFICIAL].
- **Confidence:** n/a.

### Octoculus / Blind Octoculus
- **Zones/multipliers:** NOT DOCUMENTED for the regular/Blind variants. Health increased Oct 2023 [OFFICIAL]. Weakness (Boss): Monster Damage perk [COMMUNITY].
- **Confidence:** n/a.

### Octoculus Boss
- **Zones — the clearest weak-point system in the game:** "Eight floating, disembodied **eyes** hover above its head… **Striking these eyes causes them to vanish, weakening its defenses**" [COMMUNITY — Forsaken Octoculus Boss page, rev 3 Apr 2025, via fetched diff]. Implication: eyes are a destructible weak-point layer over a defended body; no numeric multiplier published.
- **Multipliers:** NOT DOCUMENTED.
- **Other:** attacks Bite / Fireball / Consume (Consume = death + auto-revive) [COMMUNITY info card, index].
- **Confidence:** eyes-as-weak-points — medium-high (detailed wiki text; no second source).

### Lost Soul
- Flaming horned skull that detonates; "Lost Soul enemy eyes not disappearing when dead" fix [OFFICIAL May 2024]. **Zones/multipliers: NOT DOCUMENTED.**

### Blob
- Vilehalls critter; leaps onto a player's face, obscuring vision; attacks Slobber / Jump and Slime; Weakness: none listed [COMMUNITY — Forsaken Blob page via index, rev 10 Aug 2025]. **Zones/multipliers: NOT DOCUMENTED.**

### Crystal Guardians (Crystal Hunt)
- **Zones/multipliers:** NOT DOCUMENTED. **Mechanic instead of zones:** normally **invulnerable**; players must "enter their realm" via blue ground pads/triangles to be able to damage them [COMMUNITY — Fandom; corroborated by UploadVR review, fetched: "Normally invulnerable… enter their realm by approaching blue triangles on the ground"]. Fandom describes blue spectral skeletons + red spectral mages that turn blue (vulnerable) on the pads. Officially: "Crystal Guardian visuals/colliders not toggling correct" and "Crystal Guardians not attacking the pyramid" fixes (2024) confirm a collider-toggle invulnerability state.
- **Confidence:** invulnerability mechanic — high (three sources incl. official fixes).

### Crypt Guardians / "Crypt Guard" (Tomb Extraction, 21 Sep 2026)
- New patrolling enemy: hears voice chat and magic, investigates sounds, alerts other enemies, shows an awareness icon [OFFICIAL Othergate; Steam/UploadVR shorten the name to Crypt Guard]. **Zones/multipliers: NOT DOCUMENTED** — the update predates any wiki coverage found. Interaction notes: Skull Dagger "breaks when used on a guard"; Crypt Hammer one-hit-kills ordinary skeletons "(not guards)" [OFFICIAL].
- **Confidence:** existence/behaviour — high (official); hitboxes — none.

### Death!
- "He can't be killed but can be avoided" [OFFICIAL Dec 2024]; spawns Tier 6–7 [OFFICIAL Jan 2025]. **No hitbox data — by design it has no health interaction documented.**

### Mimic / Sewer Tentacles / Loot Goblin
- Mimic: chest monster; detection exploit fixed Dec 2025 [OFFICIAL]. Tentacles: water hazards, more spawns Jan 2025 [OFFICIAL]. Loot Goblin: monster class [OFFICIAL]; spawn invulnerability Jan 2026 [OFFICIAL]. **Zones/multipliers: NOT DOCUMENTED for all three.**
